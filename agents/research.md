# Research

## Role

Writes a short company brief before an application or an interview.

## Inputs

- A posting selected by the Coordinator

## Outputs

- A brief covering the product, the culture, relevant people and smart questions to ask

## Hands off to

- Coordinator, which passes the brief to Tailor and Interview Coach

## Rules it enforces

- Uses public sources only.
- Keeps the brief short and practical.
